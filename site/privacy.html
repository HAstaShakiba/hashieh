Privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Hashieh is a free, open-source notes app for Windows, Linux and macOS. This page explains what data it uses. The source code is public on GitHub, so you can check everything described here.

Your notes stay with you

Hashieh stores your folders, notes, pictures and settings on your own computer. There is no Hashieh account and no Hashieh server. Hashieh doesn't collect analytics, crash reports or any other data, and the developer never receives your notes.

Google sync (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Google, Hashieh syncs your notes between your computers through your own Google Drive. Hashieh asks Google for these permissions only:

  • See, create and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). Hashieh keeps your folders, notes and pictures in a hidden app folder in your Drive. It can't see or change any of your other Drive files.
  • Your email address (openid, email). Hashieh shows it in its settings so you know which account is syncing.

The data goes straight between your computer and Google. The developer has no access to it. Hashieh keeps a sign-in token in its settings folder on your computer so it can sync without asking you to sign in every time.

How Google user data is used

Hashieh uses data from Google only to sync your notes between your own devices and to show which account is signed in. It doesn't share this data with anyone, doesn't use it for advertising and doesn't use it to train AI or machine-learning models. Hashieh's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping sync and deleting data

  • In Hashieh, open ⚙ ▸ Google sync ▸ Sign out. Hashieh deletes its sign-in token from your computer and asks Google to end its access.
  • To delete the synced copy, open Google Drive ▸ Settings ▸ Manage apps, find Hashieh, and choose Delete hidden app data.
  • You can also remove Hashieh's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Notes on your computer are in Hashieh's data folder (⚙ ▸ Open data folder); delete it to remove them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. For questions, open an issue on GitHub.
