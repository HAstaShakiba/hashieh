**Sync your notes between computers with your Google account.**

### New
- **Google sync:** open ⚙ ▸ Google sync ▸ *Sign in with Google…* on each computer. Folders, notes and pictures sync through a hidden Hashieh folder in your own Google Drive. Hashieh can't see your other Drive files, and nothing goes to a Hashieh server.
- Hashieh syncs when it starts, every 5 minutes, when you hide the panel, and when you click *Sync now*.
- When the same note changed on two computers, the later change wins. Deleted notes and folders stay deleted on every computer, unless a note was edited somewhere else after it was deleted.
- Command line: `hashieh sync`, `hashieh sync status`, `hashieh sync login` and `hashieh sync logout`.
- [Privacy policy](https://hastashakiba.github.io/hashieh/privacy.html).

### Good to know
- The first sync merges the notes each computer already has, so new installs may add a second copy of the welcome note. Delete it once and it's gone everywhere.
- Changes are compared by time, so keep your computers' clocks right.
- *Sign out* stops syncing on that computer. Your notes stay on it and in Drive. To delete the Drive copy, open Google Drive ▸ Settings ▸ Manage apps ▸ Hashieh ▸ *Delete hidden app data*.
- Your notes database gets two new columns the first time 0.3.0 starts. Older versions can still open it.
